# Senior Physical Demands Audit

## Audit Methodology
All 10 recipes audited for physical demands on seniors (60+). Focus areas: heavy lifting, hot liquid handling, repetitive motions, prolonged standing, hard-to-grip tasks. Each recipe rated Low/Moderate/High with modifications suggested.

---

## 1. Kitchari
**Physical Demand:** LOW
**Tasks:** Rinsing grains, measuring spices, sautéing, simmering
**Concerns:** Occasional stirring over 40-45 min simmer
**Modifications:**
- Use pre-washed lentils to reduce rinsing
- Suggest sitting at counter during simmering breaks
- Use lightweight non-stick pot
**Safety:** Timer reminders for stirring intervals (every 10 min)

## 2. Golden Milk / Haldi Doodh
**Physical Demand:** LOW
**Tasks:** Measuring spices, warming milk, stirring
**Concerns:** Hot milk handling
**Modifications:**
- Use microwave-friendly mug for warming (no stove standing)
- Pre-mix dry spice blend for multiple servings
**Safety:** Warn about milk boiling over — use larger mug

## 3. Ginger, Goji & Jujube Bone Broth
**Physical Demand:** HIGH ⚠️
**Tasks:** Handling heavy bones, lifting stockpot, long simmering, straining
**Concerns:** Heavy lifting, hot liquid straining, long duration
**Modifications:**
- **Use Instant Pot or slow cooker** — eliminates heavy stockpot lifting
- **Smaller bone quantities** (1-2 lbs vs. 5+ lbs) for manageable size
- **Pre-cut bones** from butcher — no sawing or heavy knife work
- **Mesh strainer with handle** instead of lifting heavy pot to strain
- **Simmer overnight in slow cooker** — no standing, no stirring
- **Option: use collagen powder + goji/jujube/ginger** for a 15-min "quick broth" alternative
**Safety:** Hot liquid = scald risk. Use ladle with grip, never lift full pot.

## 4. Congee with Shiitake
**Physical Demand:** LOW-MODERATE
**Tasks:** Rinsing rice, long simmering, occasional stirring
**Concerns:** Prolonged standing over 1-2 hours
**Modifications:**
- **Use rice cooker with congee setting** — zero stirring, zero standing
- If stovetop: heavy-bottomed pot, sit on stool nearby, stir every 15 min
**Safety:** Congee bubbles and spits — use deep pot, loose lid

## 5. Saffron & Cardamom Sleep Milk
**Physical Demand:** LOW
**Tasks:** Steeping saffron, warming milk, adding honey
**Concerns:** Crushing cardamom pods (hard shells)
**Modifications:**
- **Use pre-ground cardamom** instead of pods
- Pre-soak saffron threads in warm water overnight (softens, easy to add)
**Safety:** Same hot milk precautions as Golden Milk

## 6. Low-Sodium Miso Soup with Wakame
**Physical Demand:** VERY LOW
**Tasks:** Rehydrating wakame, dissolving miso in hot water
**Concerns:** Almost none — easiest recipe in the book
**Modifications:**
- **Use instant wakame** (just add hot water, no cutting)
- **Miso dissolves in cool water first** — then add hot water, no whisking
**Safety:** Minimal. Hot water handling only.

## 7. Hibiscus & Cinnamon Agua de Jamaica
**Physical Demand:** LOW
**Tasks:** Boiling water, steeping flowers, straining
**Concerns:** Hot water handling, straining flowers
**Modifications:**
- **Use electric kettle with auto-shutoff** (no heavy kettle lifting)
- **Fine mesh tea infuser** instead of straining whole pot
- **Cold-brew option:** steep overnight in refrigerator (zero hot water handling)
**Safety:** Electric kettle lighter than stovetop

## 8. Beetroot, Ginger & Rose Sharbat
**Physical Demand:** MODERATE ⚠️
**Tasks:** Peeling raw beetroot, grating ginger, boiling, straining
**Concerns:** Raw beetroot is extremely hard to peel and chop; ginger grating requires grip strength
**Modifications:**
- **Use pre-cooked/steamed beets** from store — zero peeling
- **Use microplane grater with grip** for ginger (easier than box grater)
- **Pre-minced ginger** from jar as alternative
- **Wear gloves** — beetroot stains hands severely
**Safety:** Hard vegetables + sharp peeler = slip risk. Use Y-peeler with grip.

## 9. Black Seed (Habba Sawda) Honey Paste
**Physical Demand:** VERY LOW
**Tasks:** Mixing seeds and honey
**Concerns:** None — simplest recipe
**Modifications:**
- **Pre-ground black seed** available if chewing whole seeds is uncomfortable
- **Softened honey** (warm jar in warm water) for easier mixing
**Safety:** None

## 10. Sage & Honey Tea
**Physical Demand:** VERY LOW
**Tasks:** Steeping tea, adding honey
**Concerns:** None
**Modifications:**
- **Sage tea bag** option (zero prep) vs. loose leaves
- **Honey dripper** instead of spoon for easier handling
**Safety:** Minimal. Hot water only.

## Summary of High/Moderate Demand Recipes

| Recipe | Demand | Key Concern | Solution |
|--------|--------|-------------|----------|
| Bone Broth | HIGH ⚠️ | Heavy lifting, hot straining | Instant Pot, pre-cut bones, smaller batch |
| Beetroot Sharbat | MODERATE ⚠️ | Hard peeling, staining | Pre-cooked beets, microplane grater, gloves |
| Congee | LOW-MODERATE | Long simmering | Rice cooker with congee setting |
| Kitchari | LOW | Occasional stirring | Timer reminders, pre-washed lentles |

## Universal Senior Safety Recommendations
1. **Sit while working** — stool at counter for all prep tasks
2. **Lightweight equipment** — non-stick pots, electric kettles, ladles with grips
3. **Timer use** — all simmering recipes need audible timers (seniors may lose track)
4. **Pre-prepped ingredients** — where available, use pre-washed, pre-cut, pre-minced
5. **Hot liquid caution** — all recipes with hot liquids should carry a scald warning icon
6. **Slow cooker / Instant Pot** — recommended for long-simmer recipes (bone broth, kitchari, congee)
